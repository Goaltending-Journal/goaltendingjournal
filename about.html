About the GRJ

An open source journal for ice hockey goaltending research.

Leave a tip on Ko-fi →
The Mission

The GRJ is dedicated to bridging the gap between evidence-based research in ice hockey goaltending and the coaching community.

The Vision

Building an open-sourced sports journal that is dedicated to educating the goalie coaching community and supporting the goalie-specific researchers.

The Promise

To provide a free journal that is dedicated to sharing, reviewing, and inviting conversations surrounding evidence-based research in ice hockey goaltending.

Offerings

 BriefsFor coaches

Easy-to-digest research breakdowns that turn theory, academia, and statistics into practice design ideas for coaches.

 ConferencesVirtual

 Peer ReviewsMonthly

We invite dedicated goalie coaches to join our monthly Peer Review sessions, which help build our network of evidence-based coaches and designers.
