Why we exist

Mission

The Mission

The GRJ is an open-sourced sports journal dedicated to bridging the gap between ice hockey goaltending research and the general public.

The Vision

To build a professional sports journal that is dedicated to supporting the researchers and the goalie coaching community at large.

The Promise

To provide a free and accurate resource that continually refines its knowledge base in order to support learning, sharing, and creativity in the goaltending community.

Our learning design model

How We Work

  1. Open source on GitHub

    Everything lives in a public GitHub repository, so anyone can read it, check it and help improve it.

  2. The Oracle

    The main database of evidence-based goaltending research.

  3. Briefs

    Raw research becomes short learning modules. Briefs bridge the gap between the hard data and the everyday goalie coach.

  4. Peer Reviews

    Monthly sessions invite coaches and researchers to discuss the work. What we learn flows back into the Oracle.

    Editorial standards

    Ethics & Standards

    The GRJ covers evidence-based work only, held to the standards of a professional journal.

    1. What counts as evidence

      A peer-reviewed journal article, or an examined and accepted master's or doctoral thesis with a permanent record. Every item shows its source type.

    2. What stays out

      Conference abstracts, posters, preprints, magazines, bachelor's theses, unpublished data and blogs are kept off our evidence pages.

    3. Gray area

      A short, hand-picked list of foundational works, such as a key book, may appear. Each is labelled and never graded as evidence.

    4. General Sports

      Research from sport in general, not specific to goaltending, appears in the Coaching Oracle labelled General Sports and is never described as goaltending evidence.

    5. Verification

      Every title, author list, year, journal and DOI is checked against a primary record before it appears.

    6. Peer review

      Published studies are discussed in our monthly Peer Review session, and every Brief and GRJ analysis is checked by two reviewers before it goes live.

    7. Report an error

      Spotted a mistake? Email info@goaltendingresearch.com and the editor will read and reply personally. Corrections are noted on the page, never made silently.

    Free and open source

    Donate

    The GRJ is free to use, for everyone. There is no paywall, no ads and no sign-up. Every study, brief and stat is open to read, and the code behind the site is open source on GitHub.

    What tips do

    Tips help keep the journal running: the domains and email, the tools behind the Oracles, and the hours that go into checking every study.

    No strings

    A tip never buys a place on the site. What goes into the Oracles is decided by the evidence and the GRJ standards alone.
