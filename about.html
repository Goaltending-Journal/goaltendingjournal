About the GRJ

An open source journal for ice hockey goaltending research.

The Mission

The GRJ is dedicated to bridging the gap between evidence-based research in ice hockey goaltending and the coaching community.

The Vision

Building an open-sourced sports journal that is dedicated to educating the goalie coaching community and supporting the goalie-specific researchers.

The Promise

To provide a free journal that is dedicated to sharing, reviewing, and inviting conversations surrounding evidence-based research in ice hockey goaltending.

Our learning design model

How the GRJ works

  1. Open source on GitHub

    Everything lives in a public GitHub repository, so anyone can read it, check it and help improve it.

  2. The Oracle

    The main database of evidence-based goaltending research, kept current by research agents and machine learning that find, verify and tag every study.

  3. Briefs

    Raw research becomes short learning modules. Briefs bridge the gap between the hard data and the everyday goalie coach.

  4. Peer Reviews

    Monthly sessions invite coaches and researchers to discuss the work. What we learn flows back into the Oracle.
