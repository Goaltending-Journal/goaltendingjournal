About the Journal

A living evidence map of ice hockey goaltending research, built so coaches can put it to work.

Our mission

To gather the evidence on ice hockey goaltending in one open place, keep it current, and help coaches understand what it means for how they teach and plan practice.

There is no single, up-to-date map of what research says about goaltending. Studies are spread across sports science, medicine, psychology and coaching journals, and through theses and conference abstracts. Much of it never reaches the people who train goalies.

The Goaltending Research Journal exists to close that gap. It is free to read, every record links back to its source, and it grows as new work is published.

What you will find

The project's main parts, each linked.

Research Tracker
A working bibliography of goaltending and hockey studies, searchable by topic, decade and scope.
Daily reading
New articles on goaltending, ecological dynamics and skill acquisition, hung on the clothesline at the top of the home page and refreshed each day.
Skill acquisition
Recent research on how athletes learn and adapt, as background for coaches and researchers.
NHL Stats
Every NHL goaltender's numbers across three seasons, with save % ranges that show how sure we can be.
Briefs
Research to Practice briefs that turn a study into practice-planning ideas for coaches, starting with box control under fatigue and its interactive explainer.

How we work

Open
Free to read, with DOIs and PubMed links on every record so you can check the source yourself.
Honest about evidence
We keep what a study found separate from coaching ideas it did not test.
Living
Research feeds and NHL stats update automatically, so the map stays current.
Peer reviewed
An international panel of researchers, clinicians and coaches will review submissions when the journal launches in 2027.
