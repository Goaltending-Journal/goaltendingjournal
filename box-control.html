What the study found

  • Glove errors were largest on wide pucks near the face-off dots, with lost depth and angle.
  • Squareness (glove turned in or out) carried the most rotation error.
  • Across six repeated movement patterns, the glove drifted back toward the goal line, measurably by rep 3.
  • Higher perceived exertion went with larger position error (r = 0.55).

Kwan, Daley & MacLean (2026). "Thinking Inside the Box": Ice Hockey Goaltender Glove Position Analysis. International Journal of Sports Science & Coaching. doi:10.1177/17479541261492184. 14 experienced recreational goalies, lab setting.
