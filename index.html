About these records

This is a working bibliography, not a systematic review. Records came from two sources.

  • PubMed searches run on 10 October 2026 for goaltender, goaltenders or goaltending with hockey, and for goalie or goalies with hockey. Results were exported with titles and DOIs, then reviewed for ice hockey goaltending.
  • Web searches of journal sites, repositories and theses for items PubMed does not index, such as theses, conference abstracts and trade articles.
  • The journal's researcher registry, which tracks the published work of ice hockey goaltending researchers. Its goalie items were added on 25 October 2026.

Soccer and field hockey goalkeeper studies are excluded. Records tagged hockey-wide matched the keywords but are not focused on goaltending. Web of Science, Scopus and SPORTDiscus were not searched, so some articles are missing. Records without a DOI are older, are theses or conference abstracts, or had no DOI that could be confirmed. Preprints are marked.
