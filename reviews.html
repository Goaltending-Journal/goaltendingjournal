Peer Reviews

A monthly video session where our peers and goalie coaches gather to review the latest goalie-specific research.

  • Monthly sessions
  • 60 minutes on video
  • One study each time
  • Free to join

The Peers

Sessions are led by our peer reviewers, goalie coaches from 15 countries. Dedicated coaches at any level, and the researchers whose work we read, are welcome.

How it runs

The study goes out a week ahead. The hour itself has three parts.

  1. ReadWeek before

    We share one study from the research database, with its brief if there is one.

  2. Present15 min

    A peer reviewer walks through what the study tested, what it found, and how strong the evidence is.

  3. Review25 min

    Open discussion. What holds up, what doesn't, and what the study never tested.

  4. Apply20 min

    Small groups turn the finding into one practice-design idea to try on the ice.
