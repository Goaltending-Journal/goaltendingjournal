Goalie Masks

One mask per goalie type, in place of the goalie. Each is a modern goalie mask (fibreglass shell, wire cage, backplate, chin) crossed with a knight's helm, and its shape carries the type's one trait. The team crest sits on the forehead like a decal.

  • 32team colour sets, same rules as the knights

The set

Shown in twelve different teams' colours. Inspiration: the shells and helms are knightly (bascinet, barrel, great helm, sallet, armet, hounskull, frog-mouth), the build is today's mask (cat-eye, bar and dense cages, backplate, chin, a swoop of trim). No real mask design, player or team logo is used.

Silhouettes

Every mask in solid ink, as the 32 px check sees it. The net, burst, pucks and screen stay full size while the mask shrinks a little, so the prop that names the type still reads at leaderboard size. Closest pair: Young Gun and Style King at 0.82.
