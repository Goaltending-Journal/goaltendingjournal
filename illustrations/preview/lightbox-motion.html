Light Box in motion

Only the 240 px detail card moves. When a card scrolls into view the light box warms up once, like a viewer switching on, and the X-ray fades in. Then the type runs its 3 to 4 second loop of three key poses: A is the still, then B and C, holding each and easing between them in 0.3 s. It plays at 12 frames a second, like flipping through films.

    Motion safety: nothing flashes. The warm-up is one slow rise in brightness over 0.8 s, played once; after that only the skeleton moves. When your device asks for reduced motion, the previews don't play, and the site will show the still.

    The keys are joint-angle changes on each type's pose, so every bone keeps its length in every frame. The bar under each row shows the timing: dark is a hold on a key, light is the move between keys. Team colours are those of each type's leader today. Generic goalies only.
