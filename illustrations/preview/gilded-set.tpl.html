Goalie illustrations · Step 4

The full set in Gilded Knights

All twelve types in the style Goldy picked, then the checks: every size in both page themes, all 32 teams' colours, three kinds of colour blindness, a crest for each team, and the model sheet that keeps future drawings on model.

    The set

    Each type is drawn in the colours of the team whose goalie leads it today, at the 240 px detail-card size. The numbers are placeholders.

    Every size, both themes

    All twelve types in set order at the leaderboard row (32 px), the profile card (64 px) and the starters carousel (76 px), on the page's own light and dark grounds. Changed for 32 px: the net is now ice white with a heavy edge and the pucks have a white rim, so Workhorse and Under Siege keep their props at leaderboard size.

    All 32 colour sets

    Steady in every team's colours, with the colours the picture took and the jersey number's contrast. The three chips are the tabard, the field and the trim. The tabard is the sweater colour. The trim (crest, tabard edge, shield rim) is the first other team colour that shows on the field and the tabard for every kind of colour vision, else white. Number: white with an ink edge where white reaches 3:1 on the tabard; ink where it doesn't.

    Colour blindness

    The whole set, then all 32 teams, as seen with each kind of colour blindness (simulated with the Machado 2009 model). The types are told apart by shape, never by colour, so no two types can merge. Inside each picture, every pair the eye relies on holds its contrast in all four rows: number on tabard, trim on field, armour on field and the shield's cross. To get there, four teams' trims (Dallas, Edmonton, Florida, San Jose) switched to white and Columbus's red switched to silver.

    Crests

    The marks: one crest per team on top of the great helm, drawn after the team's nickname as a plain heraldic figure. None is a team logo, crest or mask design. Toronto gets a plain leaf rather than a maple leaf, and the star, crown, fin and bolt are generic shapes that avoid each team's logo layout. I compared each by eye against the team's own logo; a formal trademark search is a legal step I can't do, so the crests should go past whoever handles that before launch.

    Model sheet

    Conceit: each goalie is a knight of the crease on a page from a herald's roll. Tone: proud and a little playful, like a team banner. It honours the type without mocking the player.

    Turnaround: the set is front view only, by design. Every surface shows the goalie facing the reader, so the canon fixes one view and no side or back view is drawn. The proportions above are the turnaround's front plate.

    Don'ts

    Team colours: designyourway.net's NHL colour list, saved with the source in illustrations/team-colours.json. No team publishes a public brand page with hex values, so this PMS-based list stays the source of record; Wikipedia's colour module is kept beside it for comparison and differs most for Dallas. Type data from Natural Stat Trick. Generic goalies only: no player likeness, mask art or team logos.
