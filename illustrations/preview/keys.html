Key poses for the loops

Only the 240 px detail card moves. Each type gets a 3 to 4 second loop of its one trait, planned as three key poses: A is the still you already have, then B and C. The loop holds each key, eases between them in 0.3 s, and returns to A. The live preview on the right plays at 12 frames a second, like stop-motion, which suits the stitched look.

    Motion safety: nothing flashes; only the figure moves. When your device asks for reduced motion, the previews don't play, and the site will show the still.

    The keys are joint-angle changes on each type's pose, so every bone keeps its length in every frame. The bar under each row shows the timing: dark is a hold on a key, light is the move between keys. Team colours are those of each type's leader today. Generic goalies only.
