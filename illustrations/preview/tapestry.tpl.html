The Tapestry knights, refined

The Bayeux Tapestry style Goldy picked, with more definition and real goalie gear stitched in. Same poses, team colours and crests. Each type keeps its Latin caption.

    The gear, up close

    • Cage helm. The great helm now has a goalie cage in its face, with three bars down and two across, and a throat dangler hanging below.
    • Leg pads. Two face channels run down each pad, with a knee stack at the top, a boot break at the bottom, and two leather straps across the lower pad.
    • Catcher. It has a cuff at the wrist, a thumb, a dark pocket, and a laced T-trap across the top.
    • Blocker. The kite shield keeps the team's cross. A team-colour cuff now shows behind it on the forearm.
    • Upper body. Segmented shoulder floaters and round elbow cops, under the team tabard and its trimmed hem.
    • Stick. A knob at the top and a taped blade.
    • Stitching. The armour is in two wools, ochre and a darker ochre on the shaded side, as the tapestry does it. Every fill is laid-and-couched work (laid threads tied down with small cross stitches), inside a stitched outline.

    The full set

    Each type in the colours of the team whose goalie leads it today. The numbers are placeholders.

    Small sizes, both themes

    Below 100 px the picture switches to a lighter cut. The outlines are solid instead of stitched, and the fine stitching and the caption are dropped, so the figure stays crisp. The shapes, colours and crest stay the same.

    All 32 teams

    Steady in every team's colours. Nashville and Vegas wear gold, so their knights switch to blue-grey steel wool; otherwise the gold sweater would vanish into the ochre armour.
