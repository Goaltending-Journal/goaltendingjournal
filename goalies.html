      Big Data Cup

      Synopsis by the GRJ from Stathletes

      An open hockey analytics competition that gives up-and-coming analysts real data to work with and pushes hockey research forward. It is run by Stathletes with the Rotman School of Management, the Sport Performance Lab and the University of Toronto Sports Analytics Student Group.

      Big Data Cup 2027: a goalie dataset

      Data released
      4 November 2026
      The data
      81 shot sequences from one goalie, recorded in a shooting session on 6 August 2026. Each runs from the start of the pass until the goalie stands up after the shot.
      Tracking
      19 goalie joints and the puck, both at 60 Hz, lined up on one time axis. One JSON file per shot.
      Research areas
      Shot difficulty · Goalie fatigue and movement consistency · New hockey metrics, such as goalie readiness or movement efficiency
      Deadlines and prizes
      To be announced by Stathletes, along with entry rules and data-use terms.

      Submit a View

      Open to anyone

      NHL Goalie DataViz is an open, community-built library. Anyone can submit a view: a repository or a visual file that shows evidence-based research or NHL play-by-play data, with goalies at the centre of it.

      What fits

      Research
      A visual built from a peer-reviewed study, systematic review or examined thesis on ice hockey goaltending, with the full citation.
      Play-by-play
      A visual built from public NHL play-by-play or stats data, such as shot locations, rebounds, workload or save percentage by situation, where the goalie is the subject.
      Formats
      A public GitHub repository (an HTML page, notebook or script we can run), or a single file: HTML, SVG, PNG or PDF.

      What to send

      The view
      A link to the repository, or the file itself, with a title and two or three lines on what it shows.
      Sources
      Every data source, with its date range. Public data only: no paywalled or subscription data, and Natural Stat Trick credited wherever its numbers are used.
      Method
      How the numbers were made, so someone else could rebuild the view from the same data.
      Credit
      The name you want shown on the view, and a link if you like.

      Before you send

      Visuals
      Your own drawing only: no player photos, team logos or NHL marks.
      Review
      The editor checks every view against the data and the GRJ standards, may ask for changes, and replies personally. Accepted views are credited to you on this page.
